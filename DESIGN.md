# AI Workforce design system — draft
Reference: 11x.ai homepage and product page *structure*, not its copy/assets/brand.
Design sources: .agents/skills/frontend-design, web-design-engineer and web-artifacts-builder.
Palette: midnight ink #08131d, secondary ink #102630, aqua #67e8f9, mist #e3f0ed, deep text #0b2731. Typography: strong geometric sans with tight headings and open text. Aim for minimal chrome, distinctive process panels and meaningful structural dividers, not a wall of identical SaaS cards.
Homepage: clear outcome; proof placeholders; differentiated agent catalog; interactive workflow simulation; trust/governance; verified results placeholders.
Every product page: transformation-first hero, pain point, five-stage operating model, systems/approval/metrics, safeguards, CTA state [TBD] and related agents.
Treat supported integrations as intended integration targets, NOT working production connections. No made-up proof, logos, pricing, certifications, certifications of the platform, SLAs or guarantees.
Current scope: home and 9 agent pages. Other pages and final brand assets are TBD. Existing login, signup, pricing, dashboard, admin, docs and blog deliberately unchanged.
Accessibility: readable contrast, semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, button states and scrollable controls.
No production deployment without owner approval. Draft PR only.
